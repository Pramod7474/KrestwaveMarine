KRESTWAVE MARINE WEBSITE
==========================

This is a responsive single-page website prototype.

Files:
- index.html  -> complete website

How to use:
1. Open index.html in Chrome to preview it.
2. Replace the text/logo with your final brand assets if needed.
3. Upload index.html to your web hosting public_html/www folder.
4. Point your domain to the hosting.

Important:
- The enquiry form is currently a visual/demo form. It does not send email by itself.
- For a live enquiry form, connect it to your hosting email/PHP, Formspree, or another form backend.
- Replace the placeholder project cards with your real vessel project photos and case studies.

Recommended next phase:
